# Ilaara Project Overview

## Idea

Ilaara is a premium online store built to present handmade products in a calm, elegant, and story-driven way.

Instead of feeling like a typical shop, the website feels more like a curated boutique. It gives visitors space to explore the brand, learn about the products, and enjoy the experience before making a purchase.

The main idea behind Ilaara is to combine:

- a beautiful shopping experience
- strong brand storytelling
- simple buying and checkout flow
- safe handling of customer and admin activity

It is designed for a handmade brand that values presentation, trust, and a personal touch.

## Features

### Home Experience

The homepage introduces Ilaara with a soft, editorial style. It is designed to feel visual and memorable, not crowded or generic.

### Product Collection

The shop page displays products in a clean, attractive layout so visitors can browse items easily.

### Product Detail Pages

Each product gets its own showcase page with:

- product images
- product story or description
- price and availability
- related product suggestions

### Shopping and Checkout

Customers can add products to the cart and complete their order through a smooth checkout flow.

### Admin Access

The admin area allows authorized users to:

- manage products
- review orders
- read enquiries
- update site appearance
- control what appears on the storefront

### Site Customization

Ilaara includes a customization area so the look and feel of the site can be adjusted without rebuilding everything from scratch.

### Contact and Enquiries

Visitors can send questions or messages through the contact form, and those messages are collected for review in the admin area.

## Security Aspects

Ilaara is built with security in mind so the store and admin area stay protected.

### Protected Admin Access

Only approved email addresses can access the admin area.

### Secure Login Flow

The login process is restricted so only authorized users can enter the dashboard.

### Protected Sensitive Actions

Important actions such as editing products, managing orders, and changing site settings are limited to trusted users only.

### Safer File Uploads

Uploaded images are handled carefully so product visuals can be shared without exposing unnecessary hidden file data.

### Spam Protection

The public forms include anti-spam checks to reduce fake submissions.

### Payment Safety

Payment-related actions are handled with extra caution so order and transaction data are not exposed publicly.

### General Privacy Care

The platform is designed to avoid showing private details to visitors and to keep customer and admin information within the right parts of the site.

## Summary

Ilaara is a carefully designed online boutique that blends storytelling, product presentation, and secure admin control.

Its goal is to make the brand feel premium and personal while keeping the shopping experience simple and safe.
